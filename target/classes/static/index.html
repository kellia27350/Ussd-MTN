Product Catalogue

This page demonstrates the chat widget. Click the bubble in the bottom-right corner to browse categories and products, the same way you would over USSD by dialling *140#.

To add this to any other page in your app, just include the two files below - the widget adds itself automatically, no extra markup needed: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/widget/chat-widget.css" />
<script src="/widget/chat-widget.js"></script>
